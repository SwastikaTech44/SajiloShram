How it works

1
Find a worker

Search by district, municipality, ward, skill and daily rate. Listen to a voice introduction.

2
Agree on the terms

Send a request with the wage, start date and place. The worker reads it and accepts.

3
Pay into escrow

The wage is held safely while the work is done.

4
Confirm and review

Confirm the work and the money goes to the worker. Then leave an honest review.

Why people trust SajiloShram

✓ Verified workers. Workers submit their ID for review and earn a verified badge.

🔒 Escrow payment. Money is released only after the employer confirms the work.

★ Honest reviews. Only employers who finished a job can rate a worker.

🎤 Voice introductions. Hear a worker before you hire, no reading needed.
